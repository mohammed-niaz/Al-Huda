Prayers at the Masjid

Tick the prayers held at the masjid. Only these show in the app's “At the Masjid” tab; with none ticked it says “No prayers performed today at the Masjid”. Leave the iqamah empty to keep the usual time.

Announcement

The app shows two lines and switches language every few seconds; people tap it to read everything. Leave a language empty to skip it.
